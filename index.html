All about Computer Engineering

Computer engineering is a multidisciplinary field that applies engineering principles to analyze, design, and manage hardware and software systems, as well as their integration. The Computer Engineering (CpE) program bridges electrical engineering and computer science, training students to work across the hardware-software interface ranging from circuit design to operating systems to prepare them for modern industries such as gaming.

UE Computer Engineering

UE CpE emphasizes practical application of theoretical knowledge through semester and year-long projects, allowing students increasing creative control and expert feedback. By the final stages, students are well-versed in software and hardware design, covering topics such as networks, embedded systems, VHDL, digital signal processing, state machine design, VLSI, control systems, communications, and artificial intelligence.

Computer Engineering

Computer Engineering (CPE) is a branch of engineering that combines computer science, electronics, and electrical engineering to design and develop computer-based systems. It focuses on understanding both the physical components of technology and the software that controls them.

Computer engineers work with systems ranging from processors and circuit boards to computers, smart devices, and embedded systems. They design, build, test, and improve technologies to make them more reliable, efficient, and useful.

WHY DOES IT MATTER?

Computer Engineering plays an important role in modern technology. It helps develop the systems behind smartphones, computers, vehicles, robotics, communication devices, and countless other technologies used in everyday life.
