All about Computer Engineering

Computer engineering is a multidisciplinary field that applies engineering principles to analyze, design, and manage hardware and software systems, as well as their integration. The Computer Engineering (CpE) program bridges electrical engineering and computer science, training students to work across the hardware-software interface ranging from circuit design to operating systems to prepare them for modern industries such as gaming.

UE Computer Engineering

UE CpE emphasizes practical application of theoretical knowledge through semester and year-long projects, allowing students increasing creative control and expert feedback. By the final stages, students are well-versed in software and hardware design, covering topics such as networks, embedded systems, VHDL, digital signal processing, state machine design, VLSI, control systems, communications, and artificial intelligence. They can select from various pattern electives in advanced software and hardware design.
